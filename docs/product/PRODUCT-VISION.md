# Chipboard Platform

## Product Vision

Chipboard is a multi-location dealership performance platform that gives
dealership teams a real-time view of sales, goals, contests, recognition,
and operational performance.

## Initial Product Modules

1. Organizations and stores
2. Users and permissions
3. Sales tracking
4. Goals
5. Leaderboards
6. Contests
7. Reporting
8. Audit history

## Initial Customer

The first customer and test organization is BMW of Pittsburgh and MINI of
Pittsburgh.

## Product Principle

The system must never assume that a customer is BMW of Pittsburgh, MINI of
Pittsburgh, or even a BMW dealership. Dealership-specific behavior must be
configured rather than hardcoded.
